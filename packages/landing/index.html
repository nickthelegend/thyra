Open a door in your own wall.

Pinch a real wall on your Quest and drag a door open. Say where it leads. Muse Spark reads your room's measurements, never its pixels, and writes a world that fits it. Muse Image paints it once. It appears behind the wall, locked to the edges you drew, with real head-tracked parallax. Walk up to it and step through.

How a door gets made

  1. 01

    Pinch and drag

    Your hand ray hits a detected wall plane. The pinch points are projected into the wall's own 2D coordinates, so the rectangle grows in the wall, clamped to its edges and snapped to the floor.

  2. 02

    The room is read

    Door size and sill, wall size, your eye height and distance, ceiling, floor area and labelled furniture beside the door. Geometry and labels only; WebXR gives no camera pixels.

  3. 03

    Muse Spark composes

    One strict compose_portal tool call: a panorama prompt at your real eye height, palette, trim, light spill and a one-line rationale naming the room facts it used.

  4. 04

    Muse Image paints, once

    A 2:1 request, then checks on the result: returned size, seam ΔE, horizon row. The mapping falls back from sphere to seam fix to cylinder, and every run records which.

  5. 05

    A window, not a poster

    A stencil limits the world to the door; it is centred on your eyes with the ground at your floor, so parallax comes from head tracking. Real objects in front of the wall still hide it.

  6. 06

    Step in

    Walk within 0.3 m or reach through: the world floods around you, the floor near your feet stays real, and a lime door behind you leads back. Move 0.5 m and you're back in the room.

No key, no pretending

With MODEL_API_KEY

Voice → Muse Voice Transcribe. Room summary → Muse Spark compose_portal. Plan → Muse Image panorama. Every call has a request id, latency and cost in the run record.

Without it

Every Meta step says Setup required. The room is still read and shown, the door still opens, and the world is your own 2:1 photo or a committed CC0 sample, stamped not generated on the door card and in the run record.

The key lives only in the gateway. The browser talks to /api; the gateway has a per-IP rate limit and a daily dollar cap.

Privacy

  • Passthrough camera: goes nowhere. WebXR gives the app no camera pixels.
  • Room geometry (sizes, labels, distances): to Muse Spark via your gateway; kept in the run record.
  • Your voice: streamed to Muse Voice Transcribe, never stored. Without a key, browser speech recognition is labelled as the browser vendor's service.
  • Destination, plan, panorama: run record + image file on the gateway; DELETE /api/runs/{id} removes them. No analytics, no third-party scripts in the app.

Try it

On a Quest 3 / 3S

Run Space Setup, open the app URL in the Quest Browser, tap Enter mixed reality, pinch your wall and drag.

Without a headset

Open the same URL in desktop Chrome: the app runs in the IWER emulator on a synthetic room (labelled, not your room). Drag on a wall with the mouse.
